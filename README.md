# 🌴 Explore Panglao
### Interactive Tourism Discovery Platform

Explore Panglao is a modern tourism-focused web platform designed to showcase the natural beauty, attractions, activities, and travel experiences available in Panglao Island, Bohol through an interactive and user-friendly website.

---

# 📖 About

Explore Panglao is a tourism information and destination discovery platform developed to help visitors explore Panglao Island's beaches, attractions, marine activities, and tourism experiences through a modern web interface.

The platform provides organized travel information, destination highlights, and tourism-focused content that allows users to discover popular attractions, activities, and travel opportunities within Panglao.

Panglao is one of Bohol’s most visited tourist destinations and is known for its white-sand beaches, diving spots, island hopping activities, and marine biodiversity. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

The website focuses on delivering a visually engaging tourism experience while promoting local attractions and travel destinations through responsive web design and modern UI development.

- Island hopping tours
- Snorkeling and diving activities
- Marine biodiversity
- Dolphin watching experiences

- Beachfront tourism destinations
